Our Story

Jacob’s Woods and Furniture is a carpentry business founded by Jacob Nicolas Hamilton. He started this business when he was still staying in his parents’ home when he was 22(which was in 1984). He would create small posters and staple them on the street light poles in his neighborhood, then people would call in and order all kinds of home-made furniture. The business began to bloom so well to the point where he got his own shop in the town in Bela-Bela in 2007. Soon after he started to branch in Limpopo and Gauteng in the 2010’s.

Our Mission

Our mission and vision are to provide high-quality, reliable, and affordable carpentry services and to also be a trusted, leading carpentry business. We would like to be a carpentry known for their creativity, hard work and dedication to satisfy our customers.
